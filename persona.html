Persona 5 Royal: An obsession page

Welcome to my obsession page on Persona 5 Royal, released in 2022.

What is Persona 5 Royal?

Persona 5 Royal, a remaster of the original Persona 5, released in 2016 by ATLUS (10 years ago today as I'm coding this.) Is a turn-based RPG with a compelling story, and various endings. Royal was released in 2022.

The Phantom Thieves

You (The protagonist with the code name Joker) are the leader of the Phantom Thieves of Hearts. You and your friends go inside of corrupt peoples hearts and make them confess their wrongings after stealing their treasure (see Calling Cards for more).

Confidants

Confidants can be obtained by hanging out with specific characters outside of main gameplay. During free time events, cutscenes, and even during palaces. These confidants give gameplay benefits such as: debuffs, increased damage, and maybe even revealing weaknesses of opponets.

Calling Cards

After securing a route to the corrupt persons "treasure," you go back to reality and send a Calling Card to that person. It is similar to a ransom note; This makes the person on high alert, and makes their palace more strict on security measures. After sending such calling card, you and your party only have a single day to secure the treasure and destroy the persons palace, making them confess their wrongings after a certain time period.
